Mixed racks and a new seed type, from bioassay feedback on 1.0.0.

## What's new

- **Each dish keeps its own seed type**, so one rack can hold concurrent experiments. The type you
  select applies to the next dish you place; dishes already on the rack stay as they are.
- Seed types are saved with the run, named in each dish's summary and printed template (for example
  "5 wheat seeds, at least 15 mm apart"), and exported in a new `seed_type` column in the CSV.
- **Barnyard grass** replaces sunflower, alongside cucumber, wheat, lettuce and radish.

Runs saved by 1.0.0 open normally; their dishes show as cucumber.

## Download

**Windows:** download `SeedPlacementRandomizer-1.1.0-win-x64.exe` and run it. No installation or
.NET is needed. Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher, because it isn't
code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**macOS:** download the zip for your Mac (`osx-arm64` for Apple silicon, `osx-x64` for Intel),
unzip it and move the app to Applications. The first time, right-click the app and choose **Open**,
because it isn't notarized by Apple.
